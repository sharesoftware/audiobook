How it works

Three steps to your audiobook.

No account needed. No upload. Just tap and listen.

1

Pick a book

Open any EPUB, PDF, or TXT file from your device storage, or download a classic from the built-in Gutenberg browser.

2

Generate audio

The app converts each chapter to an M4A audiobook file using on-device AI voices. Generation runs in the background — use your phone normally while it works.

3

Listen anytime

Open your library, tap a book, and enjoy. Chapters are ready as they finish — no need to wait for the full book.
