Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Audiobook generates speech on your device. The app does not upload your ebook text or generated audio for text-to-speech processing. Advertising and optional online downloads involve separate network requests described below.

1. Who We Are

Audiobook (Android package com.ss.sherpa.onnx) is developed by ShareSoft. Contact: yangfengde@gmail.com.

2. Your Books and Local Data

The app processes selected ebooks and generates speech locally. Your library, reading position, bookmarks, voice settings and downloaded models are stored on your device. We do not receive your ebook content, generated audio or reading history through the advertising SDK. The app does not require an account or sign-in, and does not request your contacts or precise device location.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Do not send private book content or other sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

3. Advertising and SDK Data

Audiobook uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When these services operate, Google and applicable advertising partners may collect or share:

  • Approximate location: an IP address can be used to estimate a general location. This does not require GPS or Android location permission.
  • Device or other identifiers: including the Android advertising ID and app set ID, depending on device settings and SDK availability.
  • App interactions: including app launches, advertising interactions, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics: including app or SDK performance, launch time, hangs and technical information needed for advertising delivery and reliability.
  • Privacy choices: consent or opt-out signals and related information used to apply your choices.

These services process data for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Processing depends on your region, privacy choices, device settings and SDK configuration. The app does not send your book text to AdMob to personalise ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Your Advertising Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Google's consent or privacy message lets you accept, decline or manage advertising data choices. In supported versions, open Settings > Privacy options to review or withdraw consent, or use available US state opt-out choices. This entry appears when Google indicates that privacy options are required. Declining personalised advertising does not remove access to reading or listening; available ads may be limited or non-personalised.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and adjust advertising privacy controls in Android Settings. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising or all technical data processing.

5. Optional Online Features

Model downloads, online book search and downloads, app update checks and opening this website contact external services. Those services receive network information such as your IP address and request headers. Book search services receive your search terms; download hosts receive the requested file or model identifier. We do not upload your local books for these searches.

  • Hugging Face and GitHub: model or release hosting, updates and website hosting.
  • Project Gutenberg and its catalogue services: ebook search and downloads.
  • Open Library / Internet Archive: ebook search and downloads.
  • Google Play and Android services: distribution and platform diagnostics according to your device and Google settings.

This website loads Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser request information. External links are governed by the destination service's policy.

6. Storage, Retention and Deletion

You control local books, models and generated audio. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes app-private data; exported files or files saved in shared or user-selected folders may remain and must be deleted separately. Android backup or device transfer may retain or transfer eligible app data according to your device settings.

We do not operate an account database for your books or reading history. Google and other providers retain their collected information according to their own policies and controls; uninstalling Audiobook does not automatically delete information already held by those providers. Use their privacy controls or contact them for relevant requests. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and applicable obligations.

7. Age and Children's Privacy

Audiobook is not directed at children under 13. Versions with the advertising age choice ask only whether you are at least 16. The answer is stored locally; no birth date is requested. If you select under 16, decline to answer or have not answered, those versions do not initialise or load advertising and keep reading and listening available. You can change this choice in Settings. Older versions may not include this control; update to the latest available version to receive it.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at the address below so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Requests and Policy Updates

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us for information we hold, and use the relevant provider's controls for data it holds. We will explain when a request needs to be directed to a provider. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date; any consent required for changed processing will be requested separately.
